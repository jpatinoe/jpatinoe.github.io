<span class="page-greeting">About</span>


I'm a mathematician working in **dynamical systems**, with an emphasis on numerical computation, bifurcation analysis, and the geometry of chaos. I completed my PhD at the University of Auckland, and I have taught university mathematics in Argentina and New Zealand.
