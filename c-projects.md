### Current work
My thesis, **“Transitions to Wild Chaos in a Four-Dimensional Lorenz-like System”**, studies how high-dimensional ODEs can produce robust chaotic behaviour, and how global bifurcations organise that behaviour in parameter space.

- Posters / research snapshots: [Figshare profile](https://auckland.figshare.com/authors/Juan_Patino_Echeverria/12040190)
