Ingooz

Ingooz was an online education platform connecting students and tutors across Latin America to support learning in mathematics and other STEM subjects between 2018 and 2020.

📚 We coordinated over 150 students and 30 tutors, offering personalised and group instruction in topics ranging from algebra and calculus to programming and statistics.

The platform is no longer active, but this page is preserved to showcase the project and its goals.
